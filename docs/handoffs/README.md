# Handoffs

The working-out behind a day of work, kept where it was long enough that the
commits and the record that came out of it leave the question unanswered: what
was measured, what was tried and dropped, and what was still open at the end.

Each note is dated and stays as it was written — it says what was known that
day, which is what makes it worth reading later. A decision that came out of one
lives in [`adr/`](../adr/), and that is the thing to read first; a handoff is
the evidence under it.

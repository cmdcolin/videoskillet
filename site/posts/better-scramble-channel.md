## A turbo version

Real scrambled channels on YouTube lose sync far harder. The
[turbo sync loss](/app/#preset=turboSyncLoss&iurl=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%92_1984_-_1.jpg)
preset swings the line oscillator a few tens of hertz either side of the line
rate and loosens both holds.

![The 1984 catalogue cover with bands of the picture slid sideways and wrapped round the edge. The grid of swimsuit photos is torn into wavy columns, and the colour is flooded pink, green and violet.](/blog/scramble-turbo-sync-loss.webp)

## What made the colour look right

Cable scramblers removed the timing marks and left the colour alone, and faults
added to the colour directly looked fake. Without timing marks the TV misreads
each line's colour reference, and it averages that reference over dozens of
lines, so one bad reading tints a whole band.

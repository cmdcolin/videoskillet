What happens when you point a camera at a scrambled channel? We had to find out,
and we made three presets from what we saw! Try
[paper dolls](/app/#preset=paperDolls&vurl=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/02/ARTIFACTS_-_Fashion_Film.webm/ARTIFACTS_-_Fashion_Film.webm.480p.vp9.webm).
We took the picture at the top from about 35 seconds into the film.

For two of the presets we pointed a camera at a TV showing the
[scrambled channel](/blog/better-scramble-channel/) and sent the camera's
picture back into the signal before the scrambler, so each lap scrambles the
last one. For the third we played the channel from a dubbed tape, like a
bootleg.

## Paper dolls

We made paper dolls from Zenith's
[SSAVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_encryption) scrambler, which
flipped the picture to a negative on a timer. We mix the camera back in at 70%
and only lightly weaken the sync pulses that tell the TV where each line starts,
so the TV keeps hold of the picture. Every trip around the loop adds another
sideways-shifted copy of the picture, and the copy turns negative while the
scrambler is inverting the picture, so the faces repeat in alternating colours
like a chain of paper dolls.

![The model from a 1984 swimwear catalogue repeated across the frame as overlapping copies, some blue and some orange, beside the grid of swimsuit photos in the same two colours. A white band and a black bar run down the right edge.](/blog/scramble-paper-dolls.webp)

For the picture above, we ran the same preset over
[a still photo](/app/#preset=paperDolls&iurl=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%92_1984_-_1.jpg).

## Flowing ribbons

We made a look that turns the picture into swirling ribbons of colour, like ink
on marbled paper, so we called it
[marbled channel](/app/#preset=marbledChannel&vurl=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/0/02/ARTIFACTS_-_Fashion_Film.webm/ARTIFACTS_-_Fashion_Film.webm.480p.vp9.webm).
We mix the camera back in at 28% over the full scrambled channel. The TV loses
lock on every pass, which means it can no longer tell where each line of the
picture starts, so each lap tears the picture again and the tears pile up into
flowing ribbons.

![Two faces at the top right of a picture torn into flowing ribbons of green, violet, blue and orange. Black, grey and white bands wind down the left of the frame.](/blog/scramble-marbled-film.webp)

## A taped copy

We also made a bootleg of the scrambled channel,
[bootleg fight](/app/#preset=bootlegFight&iurl=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%92_1984_-_1.jpg).
We dubbed it to VHS twice, making a copy of a copy that looks worse each time,
and gave it tape noise, dropouts (white flecks where the tape lost the signal),
colour-under jitter (colour that smears and shifts, because VHS records it
separately from the brightness) and time-base wow (the picture wobbling sideways
as the tape speed drifts).
